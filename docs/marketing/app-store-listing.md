## Description (full — App Store; use line breaks)

Photo Recipes is a field camera that helps you shoot it right — not edit it later.

AUTO OPTIMIZE
Tap Auto Optimize on the live viewfinder. The agent senses light and motion, matches a photography recipe from your skill library, applies camera settings you can see (before → after), verifies exposure, and tells you when you’re Ready to capture. Optional pan left/right cues help you reframe.

PHOTOGRAPHY SKILL LIBRARY
Classic field techniques — depth of field, panning, motion blur, HDR, low angle, and more — as recipes with dials, steps, and checklists. Browse free anytime.

VOICE + FROM VIEWFINDER
Dictate the scene or use a From viewfinder note. Hands stay on the camera; the agent uses your note while optimizing.

TEACH MODE
Ask “Why this?” after a run. See the recipe choice, what changed, and what to watch for — instructor-at-your-shoulder, not a chat wall.

FREE PEEK vs PRO
• Free Peek: browse recipes, live viewfinder, 1 Auto Optimize per day
• Photo Recipes Pro: unlimited Auto Optimize, manual dials, apply recipes to the live session, full Teach mode, interactive field checklists

Pricing: $7.99/month or $59.99/year (best value), both with a 7-day free trial. Subscriptions via Apple In-App Purchase.

Photo Recipes is for enthusiast photographers who want better settings in the field — travel, landscape, workshop practice — not beauty filters or sky replacement.

Privacy: Camera and photo library access are used for capture, Auto Optimize, and optional scene photos. See the privacy policy in the app.
