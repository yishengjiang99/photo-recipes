# Photo Recipes — Lead Gen & Outreach Playbook

Actionable playbook for finding enthusiasts, capturing leads, and converting them to Free Peek → Ask → Pro trial.

---

## 1. Where to hang out

### Online

| Place | How to show up | Pitch style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reddit (photo / landscape / travel / AskPhotography) | Answer technique questions first | Soft; link only when asked or per rules |
| Discord photo servers | Help in critique / technique channels | Magnet after trust |
| Facebook groups (local clubs, workshop alumni) | Value posts + PDF magnet | Friendly, not salesy |
| Micro-creator DMs (5k–80k) | Compliment specific work → offer Pro trial | Short, personal |
| Newsletter / blog replies | Comment with expertise | Relationship first |
| Workshop instructor email | Alumni follow-up partner | Mutual value |

### Offline

| Place | Play |
| --- | --- |
| Camera clubs / meetup nights | 2-min demo + QR to Free Peek / PDF |
| Workshop wrap-ups | Leave “5 Field Recipes” cards |
| Photo walks | Be the person with the checklist; share QR |
| Local camera stores (board / night events) | Ask to leave cards if allowed |

## 3. Outreach scripts

### Reddit — comment (value-first)

> Solid question — for [technique], the field version I use is: [2–4 concrete steps].  
> If you want it as a checklist on your phone, I keep a free browse + 1 Ask/day tool called Photo Recipes — happy to share the link if useful.

### Reddit — post (when allowed)

> I put together a free “5 Field Recipes” PDF (HDR / panning / DoF / motion blur / low angle) for people who blank on the trail after workshops.  
> Comment or DM if you want it — also browsing the recipes free online. Not a filter app; actual shooting steps.

### Creator DM

> Hey [Name] — loved your [specific shot / reel] especially [detail].  
> I built Photo Recipes: field checklists + Ask for the scene (not filters).  
> Would you try Pro free for 7 days and tell me what breaks? Happy to comp a month if you post an honest take. No script required.

### Workshop instructor email

Subject: Post-workshop pocket recipes for your alumni

> Hi [Name],  
> I help workshop alumni keep technique on their phone after the weekend — field recipes (dials, steps, checklists) + Ask for the scene in front of them.  
> Would you share a free “5 Field Recipes” PDF with your last cohort, or bundle a Pro trial code in your follow-up email? Happy to customize the PDF with your workshop name.  
> Thanks,  
> [You]

### Camera club note

> Quick share for members who’ve moved past Auto: Photo Recipes is a pocket field guide (browse free, 1 Ask/day free). I can drop QR cards + a free PDF at the next meeting if useful.

### Follow-up (no reply in 5–7 days)

> Circling back once — totally fine if it’s a no. If useful, here’s the free PDF / Free Peek link: [link]. Either way, appreciate the work you do for [community / students / audience].

## 6. Compliance & etiquette

- Follow each community’s self-promo rules; prefer value posts.
- No scraped email spam; personalize every DM.
- Disclose affiliation when pitching product.
- Don’t imply endorsement by workshops or creators without OK.
- Respect “no” — mark closed-lost; don’t triple-message.
- GDPR/CAN-SPAM basics if you email the magnet list (opt-in, unsubscribe).
