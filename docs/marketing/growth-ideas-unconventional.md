# Photo Recipes — Unconventional Growth Ideas

60 ideas deliberately **not** “post more on Reddit” or “tweak Meta interests.” Use for experiments after foundations are live.

---

## Acquisition angles (1–12)

1. **Golden-hour SMS club** — opt-in texts on local sunset with one recipe chip + Ask link.
2. **National park entrance partnership** — QR on “photo tips” flyer at visitor centers (permit-friendly).
3. **Airline seatback / travel zine** — one-page “shoot out the window” recipe.
4. **Camera strap tag** — tiny QR: “Forgot the settings? Ask.”
5. **Weather app overlay** — “Foggy AM → try this recipe” deep link (partner or widget).
6. **Strava / hiking app crossover** — trail-head photo recipe packs.
7. **Airbnb Experiences hosts** — photography hosts hand PDF magnet to guests.
8. **Used-camera counter cards** — “Past Auto?” cards at refurb desks.
9. **Telescope / astro nights** — motion blur & long-exposure recipes at star parties.
10. **Wedding second-shooter kit** — pocket recipes for low light / motion.
11. **Ski resort photo spots** — panning recipes at gondola lines.
12. **Ferry / train scenic routes** — motion + framing recipes on route maps.

## Lead magnets (13–21)

13. **Seasonal 4-pack** — Spring bloom / summer haze / fall color / winter exposure PDFs.
14. **“One lens challenge”** — 7 recipes for a single prime.
15. **Kid-chaos pack** — motion + low light for parents (ethical, non-creepy framing).
16. **City night walk** — 5 urban recipes + map pins.
17. **Workshop alumni “forget-me-not”** — branded PDF for instructors.
18. **Printable DOF wheel** companion → digital Ask upsell.
19. **Wallpaper set** — lock screen checklist + Free Peek URL.
20. **Voice-memo Ask tutorial** — 60s audio: how to describe a scene.
21. **Postcard pack** — physical recipes sold/given at clubs; QR on back.

## Partnerships (22–30)

22. **Lens rental houses** — recipe card in every rental bag.
23. **Photo lab / print shop** — counter QR for technique, not editing.
24. **Insurance / gear protect brands** — co-content “don’t miss the shot.”
25. **Tour companies** — sunrise tour upsell: field recipes.
26. **University photo clubs** — campus ambassador Pro codes.
27. **Library makerspaces** — talk + PDF; leave bookmarks.
28. **National Trust / land trusts** — ethical landscape recipe guides.
29. **Film renaissance shops** — hybrid film+digital exposure recipes.
30. **Drone clubs** — “same scene, two recipes” stills vs aerial (stay in stills lane).

## Content formats (31–40)

31. **Before-settings / after-settings** carousels (not before/after filters).
32. **“Describe this scene”** interactive Stories → Ask CTA.
33. **Silent field film** — 15s, no VO, only checklist overlays.
34. **Mistake autopsy** — soft critique of common wrong dials (own photos).
35. **Recipe vs ChatGPT** blind test videos (respectful, factual).
36. **Instructor takeover** — guest posts one recipe/week.
37. **ASMR dial clicks** + checklist ASMR (odd but sticky).
38. **Comic strip** — character who always forgets shutter speed.
39. **Live golden-hour Ask** — weekly stream: audience scenes → recipes.
40. **Zine drop** — quarterly printed mini-zine to magnet list VIPs.

## Growth experiments (41–60)

41. **Pay-what-you-want day** once/quarter for Pro annual.
42. **Gift a trial** — users send 7-day trial to a shoot buddy.
43. **Duo mode** — two phones on a photo walk; shared recipe session.
44. **Recipe roulette** — daily random technique challenge.
45. **Streak: 7 Asks / 7 days** → badge + yearly discount.
46. **Local leaderboard** — most field Asks by city (opt-in, privacy-safe).
47. **“Replace your screenshot folder”** migration tool — import notes → recipes.
48. **Shortcut / Siri phrase** — “Photo recipe for panning.”
49. **Watch complication** — aperture/shutter reminder + deep link.
50. **NFC sticker kit** — tap bag → today’s recipe.
51. **Geofenced tips** — famous overlooks trigger relevant recipe (opt-in).
52. **B2B “team Pro”** for workshop schools (seat licenses).
53. **Critique swap** — Ask recipe + human critique marketplace (later).
54. **Recipe licensing** — let instructors publish packs (rev share).
55. **Exit-survey scholarship** — free year for student portfolio winners.
56. **Lost-shot memorial** — user stories of missed settings → product aha.
57. **Pop-up “settings clinic”** at festivals — 10-min Ask demos.
58. **Bundle with physical checklist cards** (SKU on Etsy / club).
59. **Translation crowdsource** — community translates recipes → new markets.
60. **Anti-challenge** — “No chimping for 1 hour; use recipes only” event.
