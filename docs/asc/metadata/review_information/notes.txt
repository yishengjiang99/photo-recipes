ProTune Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (group "Grepawk Photos Pro"): com.ragnus.mvp.pro.yearly ($59.99/year) and com.ragnus.mvp.pro.monthly ($7.99/month), each with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. How to reach the paywall: on the Camera tab tap the "Pro" (crown) button in the top bar, or open Settings > Upgrade to Pro. It also appears after the free daily Auto Optimize limit is reached. Restore purchases is on the paywall and in Settings.

No account or login is required. Purchases work with a sandbox Apple ID; the purchase is verified with StoreKit 2 and Pro unlocks immediately. Camera and microphone permissions are used only for taking photos and voice commands.
