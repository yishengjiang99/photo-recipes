Ad creatives and briefs for Meta / Reddit / short-form.

**ICP:** High-end photography enthusiasts / workshop alumni — craft dials over vibes.

**Positioning (v2):** Agentic field camera — **Auto Optimize** (Sense → Reason → Apply → Verify → Capture). Recipes = skill library. Voice + From viewfinder. Pan L/R cues. **Not** filters / AI magic / Lightroom / book branding. No App Store badges in paid statics.

**Offer:** Free Peek · 1 Auto Optimize/day · Pro $7.99/mo or **$59.99/yr** (primary) · 7-day trial.

**One-liner:** Auto Optimize reads the scene, picks a photography recipe, sets your camera, and tells you when you’re ready to shoot.
