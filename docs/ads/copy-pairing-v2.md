## Positioning lock

- **What it is:** Agentic field camera — Auto Optimize runs Sense → Reason (recipe tools) → Apply settings → Verify → Capture (internal loop; use in how-it-works only, not as marketing slogan).
- **ICP:** High-end photography enthusiasts — serious hobbyists with real cameras who use the phone as a companion field tool. Not casual filter users.
- **Recipes:** Skill library the agent (and you) can use — not Instagram filters.
- **Inputs:** **From viewfinder** scene note leads; pan L/R (and up/down) direction cues while optimizing. Voice dictate = secondary (mention once in body/FAQ max).
- **Slogan:** **Set the shot. Then take it.** (replaces Sense·Recipe·Shoot / Sense·Reason·Apply as marketing slogan.)
- **Not:** AI magic filters, sky replacement, Lightroom presets, chat-toy, beauty enhance.
- **Offer:** Free Peek (browse + **1 Auto Optimize/day**) · Pro **$7.99/mo** or **$59.99/yr** (primary) · **7-day trial**. iOS = StoreKit IAP only.

## Do / Don’t

**Do:** Lead with From viewfinder Auto Optimize; name agentic controls; coach-only for aperture/ND/tripod; ICP enthusiasts; slogan **Set the shot. Then take it.**; elevator one-liner; Free Peek; pan cues; yearly CTA.

**Don’t:** Lead with voice / “Voice it”; put voice in headlines, hooks 1–3, or PT openers; use Sense·Reason·Apply as marketing slogan; AI magic, enhance, beautify, imply aperture writes on device, Shop Now, App Store badges, book branding.
