## Elevator (on-image / primary energy)

Point your phone at the shot — Photo Recipes Auto Optimizes shutter, ISO, and focus so you capture the technique, not fix it later.

## Slogan (H2 — end line)

## Headline (H1)

Point. Auto Optimize. Shoot · Capture the technique, not fix later

## Story (1–2 statics)

1. Point camera / live viewfinder  
2. **From viewfinder** chip (lead) + rose **Auto Optimize** pill  
3. Before→after chips: **shutter · ISO · EV · WB · focus** (NO aperture as applied)  
4. Optional tiny Ready status  
5. End: slogan  

Voice/mic: **do not lead** — omit or quiet secondary at most.
