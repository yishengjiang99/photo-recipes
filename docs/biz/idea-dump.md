# Photo Recipes — Idea Dump (v2)

Harder, non-obvious ideas beyond baseline SaaS packaging. Ranked within each bucket by expected upside × feasibility for Photo Recipes *as it exists* (thin catalog, Stripe live, Ask paywall, workshop ICP).

Generated 2026-09-18 by Biz Dev for Chief of Staff relay.

---

## A. Packaging & monetization experiments

1. **Shoot-Day Pass ($2.99 / 24h)** — impulse buy when Free Peek hits the Ask wall *in the field*. Unlocks unlimited Ask + checklists for one calendar day. Converts tire-kickers who won’t commit yearly; funnel into trial (“Loved today? Keep Pro 7 days free”). **#1 packaging experiment.**
2. **Season Pass ($19–29 / 90 days)** — “Fall color / Winter night / Spring wildflower.” Time-boxed content + Pro features. Photographers think in seasons, not months.
3. **Recipe Pack à la carte ($4.99 one-shot)** — buy “Long Exposure Night” forever without Pro; Ask stays gated. Tests WTP without cannibalizing yearly if packs never include Ask.
4. **Founders 100 Lifetime ($149)** — hard cap, public counter, roadmap votes. Cash for content sprint + social proof.
5. **Student / workshop alum code** — 50% off yearly for 14 days post-workshop (instructor-issued).
6. **“Plus one” field partner** — Pro yearly includes 1 free guest login for a shooting buddy.
7. **Gear-linked SKUs** — “Bought a tripod? Unlock Stabilization + Long Exposure free for 7 days.” Partner affiliate links; monetize even Free Peek.
8. **Negative test: kill monthly for 2 weeks** — yearly-only checkout. Measure trial starts.

## B. Non-obvious revenue / MRR paths

1. **Instructor revenue share, inverted** — charge instructors $0 for the tool; take **15% of workshop ticket** when Photo Recipes is the official field guide (Stripe Connect), *or* $5/seat site license. A/B with 3 partners.
2. **Sponsored “conditions of the week”** — tourism boards / parks (“Shoot Joshua Tree this weekend”). $500–2k/placement once email list ≥2k.
3. **B&H / Adorama affiliate layer** — every recipe gear checklist deep-links with tags. Non-SaaS MRR that funds SaaS CAC.
4. **White-label PDF “trip cards”** — printable pocket cards; $9 pack or include in Pro. Physical artifact in camera bag → open app.
5. **Certification lite** — “Field Fundamentals” badge after 12 checklists + 3 Ask reflections. Sell to clubs as $49 group challenge.
6. **Data licensing (later)** — anonymized technique-failure insights; park until 10k Ask events.
7. **Corporate offsite photo days** — high ACV, rare; park behind workshop channel.

### Stack math (aggressive but concrete)

- 40 Shoot-Day Passes/week × $3 ≈ $480 → nurture 15% to yearly ≈ +6 Pro/week
- 8 workshop weekends/month × 15 seats × $5 license = $600
- Gear affiliate at 4% of $200 AOV × 50 clicks/week converting 8% ≈ ~$320/mo early

Stack these under the Pro base — don’t wait for 1,250 pure subscribers.

## C. BD / partnerships (weirder than “email Canon”)

### Pursue

1. National park photography permit holders / guide concessionaires
2. Used-camera discounters (**KEH, MPB**) — “New-to-you body? 30 days Pro free”
3. Photo lab / print shops (local + Mpix) — counter QR
4. Astronomy clubs + blue-hour / Milky Way packs
5. Drone technique creators — same physics, underserved
6. Weather app co-promo — storm → motion blur / HDR recipes
7. Airbnb Experience photo hosts
8. University photo clubs — $99/semester license

### Ignore harder

Camera OEM BD, filter-app cross-promos, generic AI-tool directories, TikTok influencers with no technique depth.

### BD wedge script

> I’ll build your next workshop’s 5-recipe field kit in 48 hours and give every student a tracked alum code. You look prepared; I earn on upgrades. No upfront cost.

## D. Retention loops that aren’t “send a newsletter”

1. **Pre-alarm shoot brief** — user sets “shoot Saturday 6am”; Friday 8pm push with 3 recipes + checklist + weather stub
2. **After-shoot debrief (2 min)** — which recipe, what failed → feeds technique graph
3. **Buddy challenge** — share trip pack; both complete checklists → unlock bonus recipe
4. **Failure-mode library** — debug ego, not pep talks
5. **Camera body profiles** — APS-C / m4/3 / FF / phone adjusts dials (switching cost)
6. **Seasonal streak: 8 weekends** — miss 2 → soft win-back with 24h Pass
7. **EXIF import** (with vision) — “you shot f/5.6; recipe wanted f/11 — here’s why”
8. **Offline-first PWA pack download** — national park trips with no signal

## E. Product/BD hybrids

1. **“Workshop in a Zip”** — $79 generator for instructors: location tags → trip pack + landing page + alum codes. Sell *infrastructure for teachers*. **Sleeper business model.**
2. **Recipe marketplace** — vetted pros publish; 70/30 split. Gate quality hard.
3. **Live “Grok + human” office hours** — $19 drop-in; Pro gets 1 free seat / month
4. **Location Drops** — community “shoot here” pins with recipe attach; moderate hard
5. **Anti-AI positioning stunt** — “We recommend technique, not fake skies.” PR vs generative photo tools
