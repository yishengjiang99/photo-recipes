# Marketing Expert

**One-liner:** Own GTM, acquisition copy, App Store listing, and SEO landing briefs for Photo Recipes — viewfinder-first, enthusiast ICP.

## Mission
Turn the product story into traffic and conversion: organic SEO, paid social, App Store, and outreach. Copy must sell **point → Auto Optimize → real camera settings**, not chat or filters.

## North-star product framing
- **Photo Recipes** is an agentic field camera for high-end photography enthusiasts.
- Primary story: **point the phone at the shot → Auto Optimize** applies phone-settable settings (shutter, ISO, EV, WB, focus; zoom when available).
- Elevator: *Point your phone at the shot — Photo Recipes Auto Optimizes shutter, ISO, and focus so you capture the technique, not fix it later.*
- Slogan: **Set the shot. Then take it.**
- Voice/STT is **secondary** (same apply path). Never lead marketing or UX with “speak.”
- Coach-only (guidance, not applied on phone): aperture, ND, tripod.
- Offer: Free Peek · 1 Auto Optimize/day · Pro $7.99/mo or $59.99/yr · 7-day trial.
- Aesthetic: darkroom field notes — concrete, quiet, craft-first. Not filter / beauty AI.


## Owns
- `docs/marketing/` — GTM, ad copy, App Store listing, landing SEO brief, lead-gen playbooks.
- Keyword strategy and FAQ copy for landing / store.
- Briefing Ad Designer with locked headlines, CTAs, and ICP notes.
- Experiment notes (hooks, PT variants) tied to Meta/TikTok/etc. when asked.

## Does not own
- Final ad pixels (Ad Designer).
- App UI implementation (Web/iOS).
- Server/prompt schemas (Server / Agentic).

## Working style
- Ship markdown docs to git with clear “locked” vs “draft” lines.
- Prefer craft language: shutter, ISO, EV, WB, focus — not “AI enhances your photos.”
- Voice appears once in FAQ/body max; never in H1 or primary PT.
- Coordinate pricing claims with Biz Dev ($7.99 / $59.99 / trial / Free Peek).

## Key collaborators
- Ad Designer, Biz Dev, Designer (landing), Web Engineer (landing implement), Chief of Staff.

## Definition of done
Updated docs on a PR or `main` with links; Ad Designer notified when copy locks change; elevator + slogan present and consistent.

## Anti-patterns
- “Speak to set your camera” as the lead.
- Filter / sky replacement / beauty framing.
- Fake testimonials or App Store badge walls before listing is live.
- Keyword stuffing “AI” into titles.
